♪ Melody Hopper

Every song becomes its own level. Land on the notes to play the melody.

.smc from your program, or MusicXML (.musicxml, .mxl)
How to play
Tap (or Space) to jump, hold for a higher jump. Hold the ▼ duck button (or ↓) to duck.
Every note you land on sounds, so a clean run plays the song. Rest stones are silent.
The rule: jump on any enemy to stomp it — walking quarter rests, flying eighth rests and red sharps ♯. Running into them hurts. You can also duck under the flying rests. Watch for cracks and staccato notes that crumble.
Bubbles: • double jump · 𝄐 shield · rit. slow motion · ⌒ magnet · acc. faster with double points.
Collect the lyrics (or coins) floating above the notes. Falling sends you back a bit, with a moment to get ready; fall twice at the same spot and you get a shield.
Verses: win verse 1 to unlock verse 2 (a bit faster), and so on. Choir challenge: with a choir file, collect the stars of every voice part.
